Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How long does onboarding take?
Typically, a complete transition takes between 1 to 2 weeks. Our dedicated support team will help you import your existing data securely so you can hit the ground running.
Is my school's data secure?
Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade end-to-end encryption, automated daily backups, and strictly adhere to modern data privacy regulations to ensure your school's information is safe.
Can it handle multiple branches or campuses?
Yes! Our Growth plan is designed to handle multiple branches seamlessly. You can manage them all under a single overarching administrator account.
Do you provide training for staff?
We offer comprehensive online tutorials, a detailed knowledge base, and live webinar training sessions during your initial onboarding to ensure your teachers and staff feel comfortable.
